What we store

Your email address, display name, optional headline, time zone, the roadmaps you create, and the progress you log (ticks, daily logs, practice items, weekly reviews). This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and is visible only to your account.

What we never store

AI provider API keys. If you add one, it is encrypted and kept only on your device, and it is sent only to the AI provider you chose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) when you use the roadmap builder. Messages you send in the builder go directly from your device to that provider under their terms.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or show ads. We don't share your data except with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for the database and authentication; Vercel for hosting the web app).

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything in it. You can also email [your email].

Children

Trailmark is not directed at children under 13.
